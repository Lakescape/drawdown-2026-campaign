## GLOBAL RULES (READ BEFORE ANYTHING GOES TO PRINT)

1. **Print is permanent.** A call script can be corrected at tomorrow's standup; 500 door hangers cannot be recalled from 500 doorknobs. Every line below is written to stay true even if the drawdown window shifts. That is why dates appear only on the door hanger (the cheap piece to reprint) and never on a yard sign.
2. **Hedged drawdown language only.** The City is "exploring" the drawdown; work is "being lined up" / "projected" / "pointing to October–November." Never print that the drawdown "is happening," and never print an unhedged date. We sell preparation, not prophecy.
3. **Real scarcity only — and print scarcity is structural, never live.** A live slot count on print is a lie waiting to happen (the number moves; the paper doesn't). The only scarcity on these pieces is the approved structural set: **2 amphibious machines locked, ~25 working days in-window, committed in July.** No slot counts, no "[X] remaining," no invented numbers. The retired "47/31" figures are banned here as everywhere.
4. **We state what we locked, never what competitors can't get.** "We work the lake bed" — yes. "Others work from the shore" — no (the source draft's tagline claims competitor incapacity; it is cut).
5. **No referral program mention.** The $500 referral credit launches in October. Nothing in this file references it. If these pieces are still in circulation when the program is live, the program lives in calls, texts, and email — not on print already in the field.
6. **No invented testimonials.** The testimonial block below is a structural placeholder. It prints only with a real client's words, their written sign-off, and their name and cove as they approved them. If no approved quote exists by print day, the hanger prints in the no-testimonial layout (spec 7) instead. An invented quote on a $2M+ street is a country-club anecdote we never recover from.
7. **Voice test.** Every line passes two tests: "Would Nathan say this at a BBQ?" and "Would a $5M homeowner finish reading it?" No exclamation points anywhere in this file. That is deliberate.

## PART 1 — DOOR HANGER

Format: 4.25" × 11", 14pt C2S cover, matte aqueous coating, standard die-cut door hook, printed two-sided. Warm editorial aesthetic: cream paper tone (uncoated-look stock or warm-white C2S), Cormorant Garamond for display type, quiet sans-serif (Source Sans / Freight Sans class) for body and fine print. Two inks: deep lake green + charcoal. No gradients, no stock-photo gloss.

Word budget: front ≤ 60 words, back ≤ 190 words. A dog-walker gives the front three seconds; the back earns thirty more.

### DOOR HANGER LAYOUT & PRINT SPECS

1. **Hierarchy (front):** image zone (top 40%) → headline (largest type on the piece) → subhead → offer strip → CTA row. The headline is readable at arm's length; the offer strip is readable at arm's length; nothing else needs to be.
2. **Hierarchy (back):** headline → body (two short paragraphs max — do not let the designer add a third) → bulleted offer list → scarcity block → shift-policy block → testimonial (if approved) → sign-off → CTA strip. Generous whitespace; minimum 0.5" margins; no full-bleed background color behind body text.
3. **QR code:** minimum 1" × 1" plus 0.125" quiet zone on all sides, 100% black or lake green on cream, links to [booking URL — dedicated landing page, UTM source=doorhanger]. Scan-test the proof at arm's length on a real phone before approving the run. QR goes on BOTH faces.
4. **Type:** Cormorant Garamond Bold for headlines and the sign-off; quiet sans for everything else. Body never below 10pt. Fine print never below 8pt.
5. **Stock/finish:** 14pt C2S, matte aqueous — matte is the brand (gloss reads "pizza coupon" on a $2M street). Die-cut hook, standard.
6. **Split print run:** print 250 first, 250 on reorder. If the City shifts the window, only the second run carries revised subhead language — the hedged copy on run one stays truthful, but the October–November pointer will eventually go stale, and reprint cost is halved. (Red-team mitigation W6 — see review notes.)
7. **No-testimonial variant:** if no approved client quote exists by print day, the testimonial block collapses and the scarcity block moves down to fill the space. Do not print a placeholder, a fake, or an anonymous quote ("— Lake Austin homeowner" is a fake with extra steps).
8. **Distribution mapping:** hangers go only on doors of confirmed Lake Austin waterfront parcels in the target cove list. Never in or on mailboxes (federal offense), never on properties with posted no-soliciting signage. Every hanger lands within the cove it's written for — the copy says "your shoreline," and it has to be true.

## PART 2 — YARD SIGNS — 6 HEADLINE OPTIONS

Format: 18" × 24", 4mm coroplast, double-sided, H-stakes. Two inks (deep lake green + charcoal on cream). Headline set in Cormorant Garamond Bold at 3"+ cap height — at that size the serif stays legible at 30 mph; below 3" it doesn't, which is why the 10-word cap is a hard cap, not a guideline.

**Every sign carries the same footer (both faces, ~1" cap height):**
> ATX LAKESCAPES · atxlakescapes.com · [campaign phone]

**No dates on any yard sign. Ever.** Signs stay in yards for weeks; the window may move. The hedged headlines below carry no dates, so they stay true. (QR codes are omitted from all six options — a QR is unscannable at 30 mph and mis-scanned at the curb; the domain is short enough to type. If a sign is placed at a pedestrian-heavy spot, the designer may add the QR at 1.25" — flag placement to ops.)

---

### OPTION 1 — The Neighbor Question (primary)
> **A drawdown is being lined up. Is your shoreline ready?**

9 words. The question does the work — every waterfront owner who reads it looks at their own bulkhead on the way up the driveway. Fully hedged. Use: default for client properties in active coves.

### OPTION 2 — The Decade
> **First real drawdown in ~10 years — projected this fall.**

10 words. "Projected" carries the hedge; "this fall" is the only time reference allowed on a sign (season-scale, survives a two-week slip; a named month would not). Use: high-traffic lake-road frontage.

### OPTION 3 — The Payoff
> **When the lake drops, your shoreline becomes fixable.**

8 words. No dates, no ask — pure reframing. The strongest option for owners who don't yet know anything is wrong. Use: coves where cold outreach is still early and education is the job.

### OPTION 4 — The Real Numbers
> **Two amphibious machines. ~25 working days. Then it closes.**

9 words. Structural scarcity, print-safe, and checkable — it's the same arithmetic the email and call scripts use. Use: client properties alongside an active job site, where the machine in the yard makes the claim visible.

### OPTION 5 — The Social Proof (CONDITIONAL — read before deploying)
> **Your neighbors booked their Assessments. Ask them why.**

8 words. **Deploy only in coves where at least 3 real bookings exist on that street or in that cove, confirmed at the Monday or Thursday standup the week of placement.** "Neighbors" is a factual claim on a small lake; one false placement becomes the story. Pull these signs the week any cove's claim stops being true. (Red-team finding W5 — see review notes.)

### OPTION 6 — The Brand Line (teaser)
> **We work the lake bed.**

5 words. Reads at 50 mph, plants the differentiator without saying a word about anyone else. (The source draft's second half — "Others work from the shore" — is cut per Global Rule 4.) Use: job-site signs during the window itself, and as the always-on sign after other options are pulled.

### YARD SIGN LAYOUT & PRINT SPECS

1. **Hierarchy:** headline only in the top two-thirds; footer strip in the bottom third. Nothing else. No bullets, no offer details, no $695 on a yard sign — the sign's job is to make someone type the domain; the landing page's job is everything after that.
2. **Legibility math:** 3" cap height ≈ readable at ~30 feet for ~3 seconds at residential speeds. At 18" × 24", a 10-word headline at 3"+ caps fits on two lines maximum. If a headline needs a third line, the headline is too long — shorten the headline, never the type.
3. **Color:** cream background, lake-green headline, charcoal footer. Reverse (green field, cream type) is approved for job-site signs only, where contrast against the shoreline matters more than tone.
4. **Placement rules:** client properties and job sites only, with the owner's explicit yes — never on public right-of-way without checking current City of Austin temporary-sign rules, and never in an HOA that prohibits them without a variance in hand. [Ops/legal to confirm current COA and per-HOA sign rules before the first placement — placeholder below.]
5. **Print run:** 50 units total, allocated after the first client-property commitments land — suggested split: 12 × Option 1, 8 × Option 2, 8 × Option 3, 6 × Option 4, 8 × Option 5 (conditional), 8 × Option 6. Reorder weekly against actual placements; do not print the full 50 before the first cove confirms.

## DISTRIBUTION & TIMING GUIDANCE

1. **Sequencing:** door hangers land in a cove the same week that cove's outreach wave runs (Day 1 = Mon Jul 27, 2026, 10:00 AM CT anchor) — the hanger should feel like the same neighbor knocking, not a separate campaign. First drops: week of Aug 3, after the Jul 27–31 call/email/SMS wave has warmed the target coves. [Confirm per-cove drop schedule with ops — placeholder below.]
2. **Canvassing hours:** weekday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM only, matching the campaign's outreach window. No weekend drops. A rep in an ATX Lakescapes shirt hanging a hanger is a brand impression either way — make it a courteous one.
3. **Tracking:** the QR's UTM and the [campaign phone] line are the only attribution on these pieces. Standup reviews QR scans and call-source counts per cove every Monday and Thursday, same rhythm as the slot counts.
4. **Kill-switch (drawdown delay/cancellation):** if the City/LCRA shifts the window, stop new drops immediately. Hangers already in the field stay truthful (hedged copy, 12-month credit line, make-it-right line). Yard signs carry no dates and stay up. Reprint the second 250-hanger run with the new pointer only once new dates are lined up — never paper over old dates with stickers.
