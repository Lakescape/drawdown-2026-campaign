## GLOBAL RULES (READ BEFORE ANY SEND)

1. **One email, one job, one CTA.** Every email below has exactly one thing it is trying to get the reader to do. Do not add a second ask.
2. **Hedged drawdown language only.** The City is "exploring" the drawdown; work is "lined up" / "projected" / "pointing to October–November." Never write that the drawdown "is happening," and never state unhedged dates. We sell preparation, not prophecy.
3. **Real scarcity only.** The approved structural numbers are: **2 amphibious machines locked, ~25 working days in-window.** Live slot counts are published every Monday and Thursday and may only be quoted as "as of [Monday/Thursday]'s count." Anything not known is an **[X]** placeholder — never improvise a number. The old "47 machine-days / 31 spoken for" line is retired — it was invented and must never be reused.
4. **We state what we locked, never what competitors can't get.** "We committed in July" — yes. "No one else can get these machines" — no.
5. **Send windows.** Weekdays only, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CT. Monday sends go out at 10:00 AM or later. No weekends. No exceptions for "just one email."
6. **Voice.** These go out under Nathan's name. Read each one out loud before scheduling. If a sentence sounds like a marketing department and not like Nathan talking across a dock, cut it.

## HOW TO RUN THESE SEQUENCES (USAGE BLOCK)

1. **List hygiene and legal basis.** Sequence A goes to past clients only. Sequence B sends only to addresses with a documented collection basis per the list policy — a cold email to a $5M homeowner from a bought list is how you become a spam anecdote at the country club. Suppression lists are shared with the SMS suite: a NO or unsubscribe anywhere suppresses everywhere, permanently.
2. **Deliverability.** Send plain-text style from Nathan's real address (no image-heavy templates for A and B). Stagger sends in batches of 40–50; don't fire 200 identical emails in one hour. New sending domains must be warmed before Jul 27 — if the domain isn't warm by Jul 24, shift Day 1, don't blast cold.
3. **Reply handling.** Any human reply pauses the sequence for that contact and routes to a rep inside the 5-minute SLA. No automated email ever answers a reply.
4. **Verify scarcity before every send.** B3 and A3 assume the target capacity case (2 amphibious machines locked, ~25 working days). If equipment lands at the floor case (1 machine, 20 days), rewrite those lines to match before the send. Live slot counts come only from the Mon/Thu published numbers, quoted as "as of [day]'s count."
5. **Data discipline on Sequence C.** [Finding], [Deposit], [Date], and [Second finding] are filled from the client's actual file before sending. A wrong or generic finding on a $5M property reads as mail-merge and kills the neighbor illusion. C3's "another client is waiting on that equipment day" must be literally true when sent — if it isn't, cut the sentence.
6. **A4 is conditional.** It sends only if the past-client wave has genuinely filled by Aug 10. If not, send A4 ALT — and only after the sales lead confirms the Friday cutoff is real.
6a. **A2 truth-floor.** A2 sends only if the Thursday (Jul 30) standup count shows ≥5 booked past-client Assessments. Below that, send A2 ALT — the social-proof email never claims a crowd that doesn't exist.
6b. **Event-anchored weekend shift.** If any event-anchored send (C1 Day 0 excepted — it goes within 60 minutes of report delivery regardless; C2 Day 3, C3 Day 7, A5) lands on a Saturday or Sunday, it shifts to the following Monday at 10:00 AM.
7. **Referral program silence.** Do not mention the $500 referral credit in any pre-window email. The program launches in October; clients are told when it's live, not before (consistent with the Master Call Script).
8. **Kill-switch (drawdown delay/cancellation).** If the City/LCRA shifts the window — a low-probability, critical-impact risk — stop all sequences immediately and send this pivot email to everyone mid-sequence:
   > **Subject:** Drawdown timing update — your assessment carries over
   >
   > [First Name], quick update: the City has shifted the drawdown timing. Nothing is lost — your Assessment and credit carry over to the new window, and your place in the calendar holds. I'll write again the moment new dates are lined up. Questions? Reply here. — Nathan

   Then rebuild every cadence around the new window dates.
9. **No weekend, early-morning, or evening sends.** Weekdays 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CT; Mondays at 10:00 AM or later. The scheduling tool enforces this, and the human double-checks the tool.
