## LAYER 5 — DEVIL'S ADVOCATE / RED TEAM REPORT

### 5+ specific weaknesses (in the board-approved draft)

1. **Live-number staleness in print.** A printed deck with any slots/machine-day figure is wrong within days. Fix: all live counts rendered as [X] with a dated-print instruction on Slide 13 and in the placeholders appendix.
2. **Roster gap.** The plan says "Sales Team (4)" but only three roles (Hunter, Closer, Farmer) are defined; the 4th rep is still being hired. Fix: [Rep 4] placeholder with owner and deadline, redistributed targets noted on Slide 7.
3. **Commission example risk.** Even labeled, a five-figure illustrative month can be quoted back at us in a dispute. Fix: disclaimer kept on-slide (not just in notes) and the framing shifted to inputs ("the comp plan pays for cadence").
4. **Unsourced speed-to-lead statistic.** "21x more likely" circulates widely but is not verifiable from our package. Fix: softened to "dramatically more likely" with the mechanism explained (Board note 3).
5. **Unconfirmed equipment presented to reps.** If holds fall through, the deck's moat slide becomes a lie the team already internalized. Fix: statuses verbatim from the Ops Playbook, "being locked" language mandated in presenter notes, update path in the placeholders appendix.
6. **Presenter notes double the print length** (26 slides → 52 printed pages). Accepted as designed behavior — notes print on the page following each slide, documented on Slide 1 and in the file header; screen viewing defaults to notes-off with an N-key toggle.

### 3 concrete improvements (applied in FINAL)

1. Added the "If the Window Moves" column to Slide 25 (12-month credit validity, timing flexibility, pivot to pre-booking, plain-language honesty) so the most dangerous client question has one scripted, truthful answer.
2. Rebuilt Slide 26 as a read-back mastery check ("state the mission in one sentence") with named escalation contacts and the Aug 3/4/5/8 onboarding dates.
3. Added "no silent failures" as a spoken line in Slide 15 presenter notes — the single cultural sentence that protects the gates.

**The drawdown is delayed or cancelled after 60+ Assessments are booked and reps have been selling urgency for weeks.** Without a prepared answer, reps improvise refunds, credibility burns on a small lake, and the warm list — our most valuable asset — turns hostile. Mitigation now in-deck: Slide 25's pivot column, the guarantee language on Slide 4 (make-it-right: refund, roll, or credit — see External Red Team round, Fix 4, which removed the unsourced "110% credit" figure), and the presenter-note instruction on Slide 2 not to speculate. The honest line is the whole contingency plan, and it is enough.
