Non-Negotiables · 1 of 2

Six rules. No exceptions.

1 · The 5-Minute SLA

Every lead acknowledged within 5 minutes. Auto-reply instantly, human touch inside five. No exceptions, no "I was on another call."

2 · CRM or It Didn't Happen

Every call, every note, every outcome logged. Your memory is not a system. The next rep to touch that lead deserves the full story.

3 · The Power Hours Are Sacred

8:30–11:30 AM is dialing time. No meetings. No email. No Slack. Protect it like the revenue it produces.

4 · End-of-Day Sync Is Mandatory

4:30 PM, 15 minutes. Numbers, blockers, tomorrow's top 3. Two silent syncs in a row and Nathan calls you — not the other way around.

5 · Assessment Report Quality

Every report thorough enough to stand alone — the client gets $695 of value even if they never hire us. No shortcuts. Ever.

6 · Equipment Lock

Nothing moves, nothing gets promised, nothing gets scheduled without confirmed equipment. No exceptions.
