## GLOBAL RULES (READ BEFORE ANY USE)

1. **The version gate is the SEND date, not the job-completion date.** Versions marked **PRE-LAUNCH** may be used through Wednesday, October 14, 2026. Versions marked **POST-LAUNCH** may be used from Thursday, October 15, 2026 onward (referral program launch, Order of Operations item 65; credit fulfillment Dec 15). A job completed Oct 12 whose email sends Oct 16 gets the POST-LAUNCH version. Never mention the $500 credit before the program is live; never send a pre-launch version after it is.
2. **Gratitude first, ask second, exit always.** Every script below opens with thanks, makes exactly one ask, and closes with the no-pressure exit. Do not reorder. Do not stack the referral ask with a review request, an invoice reminder, or a final-payment conversation — one message, one job.
3. **The ask takes under 10 seconds to act on.** All we ever request is a name. No forms, no links, no "have them call us." The client answers in one word; we do everything after.
4. **Identity discipline.** These scripts go out from the person the client actually knows — the rep or PM who ran the job — speaking as themselves. Reps personalize wording until it sounds like them, keeping the structure and the two non-negotiable lines (the program line and the exit line) intact.
5. **No scarcity in a referral ask.** Gratitude doesn't need a countdown. The approved numbers (2 amphibious machines locked, ~25 working days, committed in July) never appear in these scripts. If a referred prospect asks about capacity, that conversation follows the Master Call Script's standup-count rules.
6. **One ask per client per channel, then silence.** In-person at the walkthrough, one email, one text. If the client declines or doesn't respond after that, the matter is closed — they are a client we loved serving, not a referral lead we're farming.
7. **GSM-7 discipline (texts).** Both texts below are GSM-7 verified. Type rep rewrites directly into the sending platform and re-validate before queueing — never compose in Word/Notes/iMessage, where smart quotes silently re-break this (same rule as the SMS suite).

## CHANNEL 1 — EMAIL (Post-Job Completion)

Sends from the rep or PM who ran the job, signed by them — the name the client knows. Never from a generic company address. Send within 24 hours of job completion, weekdays 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CT (Monday sends 10:00 AM or later, per the Email Suite's window rules).

## CHANNEL 3 — IN-PERSON (During the Final Walkthrough)

This is the primary channel — the email and text are the backstop, not the other way around. Every final walkthrough includes this beat, without exception.

**WHEN:** After the last walkthrough item is reviewed, the punch list is closed, and the client has volunteered that they're happy — typically standing at the dock or walking back toward the truck. Never during the invoice or final-payment conversation, and never with an open punch-list item. The full beat runs 30–45 seconds. Acting on the ask takes the client under 10 seconds: one name.

### THE BEAT — both versions

**[0:00–0:10] GRATITUDE (say first, and mean it):**

"[Name] — before we pack up, thank you. This shoreline was a pleasure to work on, and I mean that."

(pause. Let them respond. Their satisfaction is the door this ask walks through — if they raise an issue here, the ask is cancelled and you handle the issue instead.)

**[0:10–0:15] THE ASK (one question, then stop talking):**

"One quick question — is there anyone on the lake we should meet?"

**[0:15–0:25] WHO TO THINK OF (only if they pause or say "hmm, not sure"):**

"If nobody pops to mind right away — think about the neighbor two docks down whose bulkhead took a beating this year, whoever you share a dock line with, or the friend from the HOA who asked about the work while we were out here."

**[0:25–0:35] THE PROGRAM LINE (non-negotiable wording — pick by date):**

- **I-A, PRE-LAUNCH (through Oct 14):** "And so you know — our referral program launches in October. Anyone you name today, I'll note the same day, so you're first in line when it activates."
- **I-B, POST-LAUNCH (Oct 15 onward):** "And so you know — our referral program is live. If anyone you send us books an assessment, that's a $500 credit toward future work for you — our way of saying thanks."

**[0:35–0:45] THE EXIT (always, verbatim):**

"And if nobody comes to mind, truly no worries — either way, we loved working on your shoreline."

### SPEAKER NOTES

- **Ask at the emotional high point, not at the paperwork.** If you're holding the invoice, the checklist, or the final-walk sign-off, your hands are wrong. The ask happens in the loose moment after "we're all set," not during the transaction.
- **A name is the whole ask.** If they give one, write it down visibly — notebook or phone, in front of them. It signals this matters and it starts the same-day logging clock.
- **Ask permission to mention them, every time:** "Want me to say you sent me, or keep your name out of it?" Record the answer in Linear. Some neighbors refer quietly; honoring that is why they refer again.
- **If they ask "why does it matter who's nearby?"** the honest answer: "Grouped properties on the same cove share equipment time — neighbors booking together actually saves everyone money." (Consistent with the Call Script, Scenario 1 Branch 1E and Scenario 3.)
- **If they hesitate,** deliver the exit line and drop it completely. One ask per job. The relationship is worth more than the name.
- **If they name someone,** that person is handled as a Scenario 3 referral lead (Master Call Script): warm intro, borrowed trust, never exaggerated involvement. Contact within 5 business days.
- **Same-day follow-through is part of the script,** not an afterthought: log the referral in Linear the same day, and text the client a thank-you the same day ("Thanks again for connecting me with [Name] — really appreciate the trust.").

## HOW TO RUN THESE SCRIPTS (USAGE BLOCK)

1. **Version gate.** PRE-LAUNCH versions: through Oct 14, 2026. POST-LAUNCH versions: Oct 15, 2026 onward. The gate is the send/delivery date, not the job-completion date (Global Rule 1). The $500 credit is never mentioned before launch — consistent with the Master Call Script (Scenarios 1 and 3) and the Email Suite (Usage rule 7).
2. **Channel order and cadence.** In-person at every final walkthrough (primary). Email within 24 hours of job completion, weekdays 9:00 AM–6:00 PM CT, Monday sends 10:00 AM or later. Text 3 days after the email if no referral reply, or standalone for text-first clients. One ask per channel, then silence.
3. **Tracking rule — same-day in Linear, no exceptions.** Every referral name is logged in Linear the day it's received: referrer name and property, referred name, channel, date, script version (pre/post-launch), and permission-to-mention (Y/N). Same-day logging is what makes the "first in line" promise true — a referral noted next week is a broken promise to a client who did us a favor.
4. **Launch-day sweep (Oct 15).** On the morning the program goes live, every pre-launch referral logged in Linear gets a personal call or text from their rep: the program is live, their name is first in line, and the $500 credit applies to the referral they already made. This is the moment the pre-launch promise gets kept — schedule it before October, not during it.
5. **Referrer acknowledgment.** Every client who sends a name gets a same-day thank-you text (consistent with the Call Script's Scenario 3 post-call rule). Referrals compound when they're acknowledged.
6. **Referred-lead handling.** Referred prospects enter the pipeline as Scenario 3 leads with a 5-business-day first-contact SLA. The rep tells the prospect exactly who sent them (with permission) — borrowed trust is the whole mechanism, and transparency is what protects it.
7. **Credit mechanics.** The $500 credit applies per referral that books a Priority Assessment, toward future work, fulfilled Dec 15 (Order of Operations item 65). Exact terms — stackability, what "future work" covers, how the credit is issued — are confirmed in writing by Nathan before Oct 15 (see Placeholders). Reps quote only the confirmed terms.
8. **GSM-7 and send discipline (texts).** Both texts are GSM-7 verified under the 160 single-segment limit, worst-case rendered. Rep rewrites are typed directly into the sending platform and re-validated before queueing. Do not lengthen either text without re-counting.
9. **Opt-outs.** Any STOP-style reply to a referral text is honored immediately and suppressed permanently across all sequences, per the SMS suite's opt-out handling. A referral ask never re-opens a suppressed contact.
10. **Kill-switch (program launch slips).** The referral program launch is ours to control — unlike the drawdown — so "launches in October" is a promise, not a projection. If the launch date moves, revert all channels to PRE-LAUNCH wording with "launches later this fall," and Nathan re-confirms the date before anyone quotes a month again. (Drawdown delay is handled by the Email/SMS suites' own kill-switches; these scripts carry no drawdown claims and survive a window shift unchanged.)
