**Date:** 2026-07-27

## 1. New Pillar 7 — Verifiable Truth (HARD VETO)

| # | Pillar | Test | Fail Condition |
|---|--------|------|----------------|
| 7 | **Verifiable Truth** | Every factual claim traces to a source: approved structural number, published Mon/Thu count, or documented measurement. Every drawdown reference is hedged. No competitor incapacity, stated or implied. | Any single failure = **REJECT**, regardless of pillars 1–6 |

All six previous pillars test craft. Pillar 7 tests truth. This is the highest-value addition from the review.

---

## 2. REAL-ONLY vs AI-ELIGIBLE Classification (Mandatory)

Every script and every shot must now carry a REAL | AI column.

- **REAL-ONLY:** Requires real or archive footage. AI generation is blocked.
- **AI-ELIGIBLE:** Can be generated, but must still obey the Real Proof / AI Myth law and Pillar 7.

Doctrine already contained the law (“AI never pretends to be the proof”). It was not being enforced. It is now a hard production gate.

---

## 3. Stage 7 Split — Self-Critique + Adversarial Pass

Previously: single self-critique.  
Now required:

1. **Self-Critique** (author agent)
2. **Adversarial Pass** (different agent or deliberately hostile prompt)  
   Default posture: look for reasons to REJECT.  
   Blind spots are the point of the second pass.

---

## 4. Kill / Scale Rule Correction

**Old (contradictory):** “Only booked Assessments count” + kill on CTR < 0.8%.

**New:**  
Primary optimization and kill/scale metric = **Cost per Booked Assessment**.  
CTR and CPL are diagnostics only. They never become the kill trigger.

## 5. Prompt Engineering Note (from generation strip)

“Amphibious excavator” in prose is insufficient. Models default to standard tracked excavators.  
Required language: explicit “pontoon undercarriage”, “marsh buggy”, or “amphibious pontoon tracks” + named dock in frame when relevant.
