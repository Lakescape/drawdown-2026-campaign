## 2. Removal Permits / Regulatory Path

**Key structural fact:** Unlike the other Highland Lakes, **Lake Austin's shoreline/in-lake work is primarily regulated by the City of Austin**, not LCRA. LCRA itself states: "The City of Austin has jurisdiction over such activities [repair or dredging when the lake is down] for Lake Austin" and recreation on Lake Austin "is managed by the City of Austin because it is in the city's boundaries." — LCRA River Operations FAQ, https://stagehydromet.lcra.org/pdf/roc-faq.pdf (LCRA, page dated 2005-06-28, still the operative allocation). LCRA operates Mansfield & Tom Miller Dams and controls lake level/drawdowns (COA must petition LCRA for a drawdown).

**Practical regulatory path for a small contractor (typical residential/marina sediment job):**
1. **With a drawdown (best case):** register each project at lcra.org/lakelowerings; work under LCRA's USACE Lakewide Permit (≤2,000 cy maintenance dredging); post permit notice; comply with COA site-plan exemption per address; dispose of spoil **above the floodplain**; complete before refill deadline.
2. **Without a drawdown (wet dredging from barge):** COA approval capped at <25 cy per address (navigation safety); larger volumes are effectively prohibited by COA code — expect the commercial market to be drawdown-driven. USACE NWP + TCEQ 401 (Tier I, BMP-based) for any wet work with incidental discharge; turbidity curtain/BMPs; TXR150000 if ≥1 ac upland disturbance.
3. **Every job:** verify submerged-land ownership/HOA permission; avoid mapped wetlands; confirm with LCRA Water Quality (512-578-2324) and COA DAC (512-974-6370) — both agencies explicitly invite pre-project calls.

## 3. Disposal Options (wet sediment/spoil within ~30 mi of 30.34, -97.85)

COA guidance during drawdowns: spoil must go **out of the floodplain**. LCRA: burning debris in lakebed prohibited. TCEQ clean-fill reuse is possible if material is uncontaminated (documented origin; 30 TAC Ch. 330 exemption per secondary source — confirm with TCEQ). Wet sediment must be **dewatered** before landfill acceptance (liquids are universally rejected).

## 5. Suggested Operational Removal Zones

| ID | Zone | Reach | Primary ramp(s) | Rationale / hot spots |
