## Regulatory overlay (applies to ALL associations on Lake Austin)

Dock/bulkhead/dredge/hydrilla work on Lake Austin is governed by **public agencies**, not HOAs — but HOAs add architectural-review layers on top:

- **City of Austin regulates Lake Austin residential docks.** Austin Parks & Recreation: 512-974-6700. Boat dock registration program with 5-year renewals; site plan review or exemption required before build/alter/repair. (https://www.lcra.org/water/permits-contracts/docks-and-marinas/, accessed 2026-07-27)
- **LCRA Highland Lakes Dredge & Fill Ordinance (HLDO, eff. 2022):** Tier I = written notification for small projects (<500 cy dredged or <500 lf shoreline); Tier II/III = formal permit. Bulkhead/shoreline stabilization and boat-slip excavation are "dredging" activities. (https://www.lcra.org/water/permits-contracts/dredge-fill-ordinance/, accessed 2026-07-27)
- **LCRA residential docks ≤1,500 sf:** no LCRA permit/fee, but LCRA safety standards + local rules apply; floating habitable structures prohibited. Docks over LCRA-owned lakebed need LCRA Real Estate Services permission: 800-776-5272. (https://www.lcra.org/water/permits-contracts/docks-and-marinas/, accessed 2026-07-27)
- **Hydrilla/aquatic vegetation on Lake Austin is managed by City of Austin Watershed Protection** (sterile grass carp program; ~36% lake coverage reported July 2026; possible Oct–Nov 2026 drawdown under review for shoreline work). Any contractor treatment of aquatic vegetation needs a TPWD/LCRA-approved treatment proposal. (https://www.austintexas.gov/watershed-protection/projects/lake-austin ; https://www.lcra.org/water/quality/managing-nuisance-aquatic-plants/ ; https://citizenportal.ai/articles/8531422/, all accessed 2026-07-27)
- **Drawdown windows = sales hook:** drawdowns are explicitly when owners repair docks, bulkheads, and clean boat slips. A Jan–Feb drawdown is being actively petitioned by Friends of Lake Austin / Eric Moreland Group. (https://ericmorelandgroup.com/blog/lakewatchdrawdown ; https://www.kxan.com/texas-water/lake-austin-business-owners-push-city-leaders-to-drain-the-lake-to-stop-hydrilla/, accessed 2026-07-27)
