## Ground rules
1. Existing customers first — they already trust us, carry gate codes and slip numbers, and buy maintenance. Every zone attack starts with the in-zone customer pods, then expands to look-alike neighbors.
2. Pods are the unit of work: one pod = one crew-day target AND one outreach batch (same message, same geography, same week).
3. Sequence assignment is printed on every zone card; the outreach team works the board left-to-right.
4. B2B tracks in parallel — one vendor approval at Goodwin opens 5 zones at once; assign a named owner this week.
