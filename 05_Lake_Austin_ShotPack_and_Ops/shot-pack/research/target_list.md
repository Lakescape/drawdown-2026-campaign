# Lake Austin Drawdown Shot Pack — Tiered Waterfront Target List
**Prepared for:** ATX Lakescapes (dock/bulkhead, hydrilla removal, shoreline maintenance sales intel)
**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Scope:** Lake Austin, dam-to-dam — Tom Miller Dam / Lake Austin Blvd (downstream) → Mansfield Dam / Steiner Ranch (upstream), both shores.

## Drawdown context (why these targets)
- The Jan–Feb 2017 LCRA drawdown lowered Lake Austin ~10 ft (Jan 2 – Feb 13, 2017) explicitly to allow dock/bulkhead repair and hydrilla exposure; it was the only drawdown since 2011. Sources: Community Impact (2016-10-25) https://communityimpact.com/southwest-austin/environment/lcras-plan-lower-lake-austin/ ; Austin Monitor (2017-02-07) https://austinmonitor.com/stories/2017/02/lcra-ends-drawdown-lakes-austin-lbj/ ; City of Austin memo (2025-10-10) https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=460715
- A follow-on winter drawdown is being actively discussed for 2026 (City memo above; CBS Austin 2025-10-14 https://cbsaustin.com/news/local/austin-considers-lake-austin-drawdown-in-2026-for-maintenance-despite-hydrilla-concerns ; Eric Moreland Group drawdown campaign https://ericmorelandgroup.com/blog/lakewatchdrawdown). Imagery captured in a drawdown window maps exactly the assets ATX Lakescapes sells: docks, bulkheads, boat slips, canals, exposed lakebed.

## Tier definitions (evidence-based)
- **A-tier (high $$):** Waterfront typically $3M–$15M+; deep-water main-body lots $5M–$25M; $500–$800/sqft direct-lakefront. Rob Roy Peninsula/Westlake corridor $3–15M; Tarrytown $2–8M. Gated enclaves, dock permits, Eanes ISD premium. Sources: Keenan Group 2025 waterfront guide https://thekeenangroup.com/the-source/austin-luxury-waterfront-guide-2025 ; Keenan luxury FAQ https://thekeenangroup.com/luxury-properties ; Grewal RE Group Rob Roy on the Lake 2026 https://grewalregroup.com/blog/rob-roy-on-the-lake-austin-2026.html and luxury guide https://grewalregroup.com/blog/austin-luxury-neighborhoods-guide-2026.html
- **B-tier:** Real waterfront access (HOA lake parks, ramps, scattered docks) but typical values roughly $0.5M–$2.5M; older/smaller lots, farther out, or limited true frontage.
- **C-tier:** Parks, public/commercial nodes, preserve shoreline, scattered low-density or view-only segments — context shots, low direct revenue.

## Method & caveats
- Coordinates are Google Maps place pins/address geocodes (maps.google.com), cross-checked against address-level evidence (listings, park pages). Items marked **⚠** are estimates or pins that need a second verification pass before flight planning.
- Lake Austin meanders hard: between the Pennybacker Bridge (30.3499, -97.7970) and Emma Long (30.3280, -97.8419) the channel makes a ~180° bend around the Emma Long/Greenshores point, and the upper reach below Mansfield Dam runs roughly N–S. "N/NE bank" = the Tarrytown → Mt Bonnell → City Park → Steiner Ranch side; "S/SW bank" = the Westlake → Bee Caves/2244 → Cuernavaca → RR620/Lakeway side. Ordering within each table is downstream → upstream (approximate around bends).

## Verified NOT waterfront on Lake Austin (avoid mis-targeting)
- **Costa Bella** — Lake Travis (Hurst Creek arm), not Lake Austin.
- **Austin Yacht Club** — Lake Travis (see above).
- **Scenic Brook (West)** — interior neighborhood at Bee Caves/360; no lake frontage found.
- **Long Canyon** — golf community off FM 2222; no Lake Austin frontage found.
- **Westlake Highlands** — view lots (e.g., The High Rd), no meaningful frontage.
- **Cat Mountain** — mostly lake-VIEW per Keenan; minimal direct frontage (treat as C/context if shot).
