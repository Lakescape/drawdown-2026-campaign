# Equipment reference imagery — third-party, NOT ATX-owned

Pulled 2026-08-04 from manufacturer marketing sites at Nate's request.

## ⚠️ Read before any of this reaches a customer

1. **ATX does not own any machine in these photos.** Every frame is a
   manufacturer's or renter's marketing image of an equipment *category*.
   Placing one under "our machines," "our fleet," or beside the committed
   two-Truxor claim is a false depiction. Same defect class the claim ledger
   exists to prevent.
2. **All are third-party copyright.** Wilco, EIK, and Ultratrex own these.
   None carry a license permitting reuse in ATX paid or organic media. Fetching
   them for internal reference is fine; publishing them is not, until licensed.
3. **Three carry visible vendor watermarks** — unusable as-is at any size.
4. **None are Lake Austin, and none show ATX crew.**

Legitimate uses: internal reference, an equipment-spec explainer that names the
manufacturer on screen, or a rental/sub disclosure ("we bring in a marsh buggy
for soft-bottom work — this is the class of machine"). Anything else needs a
license and a caption.
