## 4. THE SALES SCRIPT

### Opening (Assessment Booking Call)

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from ATX Lakescapes. I'm calling because Lake Austin is scheduled for a major drawdown this October — the first one in nearly 10 years. When the water drops 10-12 feet, shoreline work that is normally impossible becomes practical. We're offering Priority Assessments for waterfront owners who want to lock their place in our production calendar before the window opens. It's a site visit, full photo documentation, a condition report, and a permit checklist — and the $695 is 100% credited to any work you have us do. Do you have 2 minutes for me to explain what's involved?"

### Objection: "I need to think about it."

> "Of course. Here's what I know: we can only take 40 Priority Assessments before October, and we're booking them in the order they come in. Once the drawdown starts, our calendar is locked. The assessment itself is risk-free — the $695 applies to any work. Would you like me to hold a spot for 48 hours while you decide?"

### Objection: "That's expensive for an assessment."

> "I understand. Here's the reframe: you're not paying for an assessment. You're buying a place in line. The assessment is free — the $695 is a credit on your account. If you have us do bulkhead work, dock work, or sediment removal, that $695 comes off the total. If you decide not to do any work, you keep the report and the photos for your records. Most homeowners use the report for insurance documentation alone."

### Close: Booking the Assessment

> "Great. I have [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] available for the site visit. Which works better? I'll send you a calendar invite and a prep checklist. The visit takes about 45 minutes, and you'll have your report within 48 hours."

## 6. THE ASSESSMENT DELIVERABLE

What the client receives (within 48 hours of site visit):

1. **Executive Summary** — Overall shoreline condition (1 page)
2. **Photo/Video Documentation** — 20-30 photos + drone footage if available
3. **Condition Report** — Bulkhead, dock, shoreline, drainage, vegetation
4. **Priority Rankings** — Critical / Soon / Monitor / OK
5. **Permit Checklist** — What permits needed, estimated timeline, costs
6. **Proposal Options** — Good / Better / Best pricing tiers
7. **Timeline** — When work can start, how long it takes
8. **Financing Options** — If applicable

**The goal:** The report should be so thorough and professional that the client feels they got $695 worth of value even if they don't hire us for the work.

## 7. PROPOSAL STRUCTURE

### Good Option — "Stabilize"
- Minimum viable repairs
- Address critical issues only
- Lowest cost, fastest timeline
- ~$15K-$25K range

### Better Option — "Restore"
- Full bulkhead/dock restoration
- Sediment removal in high-traffic areas
- Shoreline stabilization
- ~$35K-$55K range

### Best Option — "Transform"
- Complete shoreline overhaul
- Full sediment removal
- New dock system
- Native planting and erosion control
- ~$65K-$95K range

**Pricing rule:** Every proposal includes the $695 assessment credit applied. The client sees: "Assessment Credit: -$695" on every option.
