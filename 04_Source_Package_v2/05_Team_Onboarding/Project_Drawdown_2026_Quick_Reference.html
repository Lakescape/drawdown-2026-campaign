The Opening Script
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from ATX Lakescapes. Lake Austin is scheduled for a major drawdown this October — the first one in nearly 10 years. When the water drops 10-12 feet, shoreline work that is normally impossible becomes practical. We're offering Priority Assessments for waterfront owners who want to lock their place in our production calendar. It's a site visit, full photo documentation, a condition report, and a permit checklist — and the $695 is 100% credited to any work you have us do. Do you have 2 minutes?"
