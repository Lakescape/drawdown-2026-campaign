## TROUBLESHOOTING

**"The AI is producing generic output"**
→ Add more specific context. Paste a real customer quote. Share a photo of a real shoreline. Name a real cove.

**"The AI is ignoring the Devil's Advocate layer"**
→ Explicitly say: "STOP. Run Layer 4 Board Review and Layer 5 Devil's Advocate before producing FINAL."

**"The AI is inventing numbers"**
→ Say: "Use placeholder [X] for any number you don't have. Never invent scarcity numbers."

**"The AI is producing too slowly"**
→ Break into smaller deliverables. Do 1.1 today, 1.2 tomorrow. Don't try to do all 25 at once.

**"The AI output feels corporate"**
→ Paste this reminder: "TONE CHECK: Calm authority. Local expertise. Real scarcity. Never hype. Never corporate. Would Nathan say this at a BBQ?"
