## HANDING OFF TO ANOTHER AI (Kimi K3 / Swarm Mode)

**What to give them:**
- The complete ZIP package
- This Order of Operations
- A prompt like: "You are a drawdown campaign execution assistant. Build me [specific deliverable] based on the attached documents."

**What they can produce:**
- More email sequences
- More ad creative
- More video scripts
- More social content
- More proposal templates
- More case study formats

**What they CANNOT do:**
- Make phone calls
- Place equipment holds
- File permits
- Hire people
- Send emails from your accounts
- Operate machinery

**Recommendation:** Use another AI for **volume production** (more copy, more creative, more assets). Use your human team for **execution** (calls, holds, filings, hiring, field work).
