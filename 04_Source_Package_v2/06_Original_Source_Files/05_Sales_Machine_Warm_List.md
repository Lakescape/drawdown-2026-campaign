## 5. Accelerated Warm Outreach Sequence (Brutal Cadence)

**Team Capacity Assumption**  
- 4 sales people (full time on this for the next 3–4 weeks)  
- Optional 1–2 contractors for pure dialing / SMS volume  
- Daily target: each full-time rep owns 40–60 warm accounts and works them hard

### Week 1 (Launch + Saturation)
| Day | Activity | Volume per Rep | Notes |
|-----|----------|----------------|-------|
| Day 1 | SMS blast to entire assigned list + personal email | Full list | Use the approved templates |
| Day 2 | Call every non-responder from Day 1 | 40–50 dials | Leave voicemail + text follow-up |
| Day 3 | Second SMS to non-responders + LinkedIn/FB if available | Remaining | Soft bump |
| Day 4 | Call all “maybe / later” and new replies | 30–40 | Book Assessments |
| Day 5 | Personal video or photo from the lake + scarcity update | All engaged | “Slots remaining this week: X” |
| Day 6–7 | Weekend light touch (text only) on hottest leads | Hot only | Do not go dark |

### Week 2 (Pressure + Conversion)
- Every engaged lead gets a call or text every 48 hours until they book, decline, or go silent for 10 days
- Daily team standup: remaining slots, conversion %, objections heard
- New “Slots remaining” number published every Monday and Thursday

### Week 3+ (Cleanup + Expansion)
- Move cold non-responders to a lower-frequency nurture
- Focus pure energy on booked Assessments → deposit conversion
- Contractors can be added purely for volume dialing if the list is large

**Daily Cadence for Each Full-Time Rep**
- 8:00–8:30 Team standup + slot count
- 8:30–11:30 Power dialing / live conversations
- 11:30–12:00 Log + update Kanban
- 1:00–3:30 Follow-ups + Assessment booking confirmations
- 3:30–4:30 Personal notes / video messages to hottest 10
- 4:30–5:00 Pipeline review + next-day list

## 7. Enterprise Techniques Adapted for High-Ticket Lake Austin Homeowners

1. **Status & Scarcity over Discount**  
   Never lead with price reduction. Lead with “limited machine-days” and “past clients first.”

2. **Diagnostic Selling**  
   The Assessment is the product in the first conversation. You are selling clarity and priority access, not the final construction yet.

3. **Micro-Commitment Ladder**  
   SMS reply → 90-second call → Assessment booked → Deposit. Each step is small and reversible until the deposit.

4. **Social Proof from the Same Lake**  
   “We already did [nearby cove / similar bulkhead] for [Name]. They moved early for the same reason.”

5. **Future-Pacing the Regret**  
   “Most people who wait end up paying more later or living with the problem another decade. The ones who sleep well are the ones who at least got the Assessment done while the option still existed.”

6. **Calm Authority**  
   High-net-worth Lake Austin owners respond to competence and calm more than hype. Speak like the expert who has already secured the machines, not like a salesperson chasing a quota.
