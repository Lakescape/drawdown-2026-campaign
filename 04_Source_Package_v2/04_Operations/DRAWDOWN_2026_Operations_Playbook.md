## 4. JOB SEQUENCING LOGIC

### Priority Ranking

| Priority | Criteria | Equipment | Timing |
|----------|----------|-----------|--------|
| P1 — Critical | Failing bulkhead, safety hazard, structural collapse risk | Amphibious first | Week 1-2 |
| P2 — High-Value | Large sediment volume, multiple structures, high-ticket | Amphibious second | Week 2-4 |
| P3 — Standard | Routine repair, single structure, mid-ticket | Conventional + mats | Week 3-6 |
| P4 — Fill | Small jobs, touch-ups, add-ons | Conventional | Week 5-8 |

### Sequencing Rules

1. **Geographic clustering:** Group jobs by cove to minimize equipment moves
2. **Equipment efficiency:** Amphibious units handle 1-2 P1/P2 jobs per day
3. **Permit readiness:** No job starts without permit in hand
4. **Deposit confirmation:** No job scheduled without deposit received
5. **Weather buffer:** 2-3 buffer days per week for rain/lightning
6. **Neighbor coordination:** Batch jobs in same cove for efficiency + social proof

## 7. SAFETY PROTOCOL

### Required PPE

- Hard hats (all personnel)
- High-visibility vests (all personnel)
- Steel-toed boots (all personnel)
- Life jackets (when working near or over water)
- Gloves (all personnel)
- Eye protection (when cutting, grinding, or handling chemicals)
- Hearing protection (when operating heavy equipment)

### Emergency Procedures

| Scenario | Action | Contact |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Equipment in water | Stop work. Assess stability. Call rescue protocol. | 911 + Nathan |
| Injury requiring medical | Stop work. Administer first aid. Call 911 if severe. | 911 + Operations Lead |
| Structural collapse | Evacuate area. Secure perimeter. Assess damage. | 911 + Nathan + COA |
| Hazardous material exposure | Evacuate area. Decontaminate. Seek medical. | 911 + Poison Control |
| Severe weather | Stop work. Secure equipment. Seek shelter. | Operations Lead |

### Daily Safety Checklist

- [ ] All PPE worn correctly
- [ ] Equipment safety features functional
- [ ] Site hazards identified and mitigated
- [ ] Emergency contacts posted
- [ ] First aid kit accessible
- [ ] Fire extinguisher accessible

## 12. TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Problem: Equipment Breaks Down

**Immediate:**
1. Stop work. Secure site.
2. Call backup equipment source (pre-negotiated)
3. Notify client of delay.
4. Reschedule if backup unavailable.

**Prevention:**
- Daily equipment inspection
- Preventive maintenance schedule
- Backup equipment on retainer
- Spare parts inventory

### Problem: Weather Delays

**Immediate:**
1. Monitor forecast 3 times daily.
2. 24-hour rain delay triggers automatic reschedule.
3. Notify client 12 hours in advance.
4. Shift crew to indoor/prep work or next job.

**Prevention:**
- 2-3 buffer days per week built into schedule
- Weather contingency in contract
- Flexible job sequencing

### Problem: Permit Delay

**Immediate:**
1. Contact permit coordinator.
2. Escalate to COA liaison if >3 days delayed.
3. Shift crew to next permitted job.
4. Notify client of delay with new timeline.

**Prevention:**
- Pre-file all permits by Sept 1
- Direct COA relationship
- Expedited review pathway
- 7-day buffer before work starts

### Problem: Client Dispute

**Immediate:**
1. Stop work if safety or scope concern.
2. Operations Lead + Nathan on-site within 24 hours.
3. Document everything (photos, notes, communications).
4. Negotiate resolution or activate contract dispute clause.

**Prevention:**
- Clear scope in writing
- Daily client communication
- Change order protocol
- Quality gates at 50% and 100%
